Old Town

Kaleiçi

Antalya's walled old town — narrow cobblestone lanes, Ottoman houses turned boutique hotels, and the Roman-era harbor at its center.

A wide, powerful curtain of water just outside Side — popular for river boat trips and a cool escape from the summer heat.

Aspendos Theatre

The best-preserved Roman theatre anywhere in the world, still used for opera and ballet performances every summer.

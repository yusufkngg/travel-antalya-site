Kaputas Beach Guide: Antalya’s Most Beautiful Hidden Beach

Antalya Travellers Must Visit Kaputaş Beach

Few locations better evoke the sensation of blue waters, breathtaking coastlines, and undiscovered beaches that tourists frequently associate with Antalya travel than Kaputaş Beach. This famous cove, which lies between Kaş and Kalkan, is one of the most photographed locations on the Turkish Riviera and a real representation of why tourists from all over the world come to Antalya.

Where Nature Takes Centre Stage

Kaputaş Beach is located where subterranean freshwater joins the Mediterranean Sea at the base of a small gorge. The water's distinctive blue colour, which can range from turquoise to deep sapphire and is always captivating, is a result of this natural mixture. The beach is immediately felt as a reward for the effort after a lengthy staircase descends from the coastal road.

Kaputaş offers something that is becoming more and more uncommon for anyone considering vacation to Antalya: beauty that feels unadulterated, unfiltered, and genuine.

The Significance of Kaputaş in Antalya Travel Plans

Kaputaş Beach is still open to the public and refreshingly uncomplicated, in contrast to private resort beaches. On either side are towering stone cliffs, the sea, and the sun, but no loud distractions or huge beach clubs. It is precisely this simplicity that gives Kaputaş its potency.

Kaputaş reminds you that Antalya's greatest luxury is its natural beauty, despite the fact that tourists frequently come for its historic monuments and opulent resorts.

The Ideal Time to Go to Kaputaş Beach

Time is crucial for the greatest Antalya vacation. Visits in the early morning or late afternoon offer less people, softer light, and calmer waters. While late spring and early autumn are ideal for tourists looking for peace and mild temperatures, summer brings lively excitement.

Another feature that sets Kaputaş apart from other beaches in Antalya is the sea's pleasant coolness, especially during the hottest part of the summer.

The Place of Kaputaş in the Travel Experience of Antalya

Kaputaş Beach is more than just a place to swim; it's a sensory-resetting stop. Many tourists turn a straightforward beach visit into a full-day coastal adventure by combining it with trips to Kaş, Kalkan, or neighbouring Lycian ruins.

For photographers, nature enthusiasts, and leisurely tourists, Kaputaş embodies the more subdued and sentimental aspect of Antalya travel—the kind that lingers long after the vacation is ended.

A Location That Characterises "Visit Antalya"

Experience contrast when you travel to Antalya: vibrant beaches and secret coves, historic cities and contemporary resorts. At the centre of this difference is Kaputaş Beach. What makes it memorable is that it just exists without making an effort to impress.

In addition to being a destination, Kaputaş Beach serves as a reminder of why the Mediterranean continues to captivate tourists of all ages, whether you are organising your first trip to Antalya or are coming back to delve deeper.

Kaputaş Beach Guide: Antalya’s Most Beautiful Turquoise Beach (Entrance Fee, Parking, Best Time to Visit)

Kaputaş Beach is one of the most famous beaches in Turkey and one of the most photographed coastal spots on the Mediterranean. Located between Kaş and Kalkan in Antalya Province, this small but stunning beach is known for its deep turquoise water, dramatic cliffs, and golden sand.

Despite its relatively small size, Kaputaş Beach attracts thousands of visitors every year thanks to its unique natural beauty. The beach sits at the bottom of a canyon where fresh spring water meets the Mediterranean Sea. This natural phenomenon is what gives the water its iconic bright blue color.

If you're planning a trip to Antalya or the Turkish Riviera, Kaputaş Beach should definitely be on your itinerary.

Where Is Kaputaş Beach?

Kaputaş Beach is located on the D400 coastal road, one of the most scenic drives in Turkey.

📍 Distance from major locations:

The beach lies between steep limestone cliffs, which creates the famous postcard view that many travelers recognize.

Entrance Fee for Kaputaş Beach (2025)

Kaputaş Beach is a public beach, but there may be a small entrance fee depending on the season.

Prices can change during peak summer months, but the beach itself is generally free to access.

Parking at Kaputaş Beach

Parking is a major issue while visiting Kaputaş Beach because it is located just beneath the main coastal road.

There is limited roadside parking along the highway.

Parking details

During July and August, parking spots fill up quickly. Visitors often need to park further away and walk along the road.

⚠️ Important: Be careful when crossing the road since traffic can be fast on the coastal highway.

The Famous Kaputas Beach Stairs

To reach the beach, visitors must walk down approximately 187 stairs carved into the cliffside.

While the steps are in good condition, the journey back up might be difficult on hot summer days.

Stair Facts

The stairway also offers several incredible viewpoints perfect for photography.

Why Is Kaputas Beach So Blue?

Kaputaş Beach is famous for its unusually bright turquoise water.

This color occurs due to a combination of natural factors:

  • Underground spring water from the canyon
  • Mineral-rich sand
  • Light reflection from limestone cliffs
  • Deep Mediterranean water

The result is a stunning color gradient from turquoise to deep blue that photographers love.

Sea Temperature at Kaputaş Beach

Water temperatures vary depending on the season. The Mediterranean Sea around Antalya is warm enough for swimming for most of the year.

Best months for swimming

The best swimming conditions are usually between:

June – October

During these months, the water is warm and the weather is stable.

Best Time to Visit Kaputas Beach

Kaputaş Beach can become extremely crowded during peak tourist season.

Visitor numbers by season

Best time of day

Facilities at Kaputas Beach

Despite its remote location, Kaputaş Beach has several basic facilities.

However, this is still a natural beach, so facilities are limited compared to resort beaches.

Is Kaputas Beach Good for Swimming?

Yes, Kaputas Beach is excellent for swimming.

However, the water can become deep very quickly.

Swimming conditions

Because the beach is located at the mouth of a canyon, waves can sometimes be stronger than at nearby beaches.

Kaputaş Beach vs Patara Beach

Two of the most famous beaches in the region are Kaputaş Beach and Patara Beach.

Kaputaş is famous for dramatic scenery, while Patara is famous for wide sandy beaches.

Travel Tips for Visiting Kaputas Beach

Here are some practical tips to make your visit easier:

✔ Arrive early in the morning
✔ Bring water and sunscreen
✔ Wear comfortable shoes for the stairs
✔ Bring cash for sunbeds

Avoid visiting between 12:00 and 15:00 during July and August due to extreme heat.

Frequently Asked Questions About Kaputaş Beach

Is Kaputaş Beach free to enter?

Yes, Kaputaş Beach is generally free to enter because it is a public beach. However, sunbeds and umbrellas usually cost extra during the tourist season.

How many stairs are there at Kaputaş Beach?

There are around 180 to 190 stairs leading from the main coastal road down to the beach. The stairs are manageable for most visitors, but the climb back up can feel tiring in hot weather.

Is there parking at Kaputaş Beach?

Yes, but parking is limited. Most visitors park along the roadside above the beach, so it is best to arrive early in summer if you want to find a convenient spot.

When is the best time to visit Kaputaş Beach?

The best time to visit is early in the morning, especially between 8:00 and 10:30 AM, when the beach is quieter and parking is easier. June, September, and early October are often the best months for a balance of warm weather and fewer crowds.

Is Kaputaş Beach good for swimming?

Yes, Kaputaş Beach is excellent for swimming thanks to its clear turquoise water. However, the sea gets deep quickly, and there can be occasional waves, so visitors should be careful.

Why is Kaputaş Beach so famous?

Kaputaş Beach is famous for its striking turquoise water, dramatic cliff setting, and photogenic scenery on the Kaş–Kalkan coastal road. It is considered one of the most beautiful beaches in Turkey.

Calista Luxury Resort sits on Belek's golden coastline, built around a series of connected lagoon pools that wind through the property down to a private beach. Rooms are spread across low-rise villas rather than a single tower, which keeps the resort feeling more spread out and residential than most of its neighbors.

The all-inclusive package covers a wide range of restaurants and bars on-site, and the resort's own beach club means you're never far from a sunbed. Belek's championship golf courses are a short drive away, making this a popular base for golf trips as well as family holidays.

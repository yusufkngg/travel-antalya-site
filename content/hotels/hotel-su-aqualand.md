Hotel SU stands out on Lara's hotel strip for its all-white interior concept, an unusual choice for a large resort, paired with contemporary art pieces throughout the public areas. Its Aqualand water park is part of the same property, which makes it a strong pick for families.

The design language feels closer to a boutique city hotel blown up to resort scale than a traditional all-inclusive — a different visual register from most of its Lara neighbors.

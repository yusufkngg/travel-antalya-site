Casa Sur Antalya occupies a restored 19th-century stone house inside Kaleiçi's old city walls, a few minutes' walk from the harbor. The renovation keeps the building's original stone and wood details while adding a small courtyard pool at the center.

It's a good fit if you'd rather stay inside the old town than at a resort on the coast — mornings are quiet, and everything in Kaleiçi is within walking distance.

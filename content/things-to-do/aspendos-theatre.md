Built in 155 AD, Aspendos Theatre still has its original acoustics intact — a whisper on stage can be heard clearly at the very top row. Every summer it hosts the Aspendos Opera and Ballet Festival, one of the only places you can see a performance in a fully-functioning 2,000-year-old Roman theatre.
